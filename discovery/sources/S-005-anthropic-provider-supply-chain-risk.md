# External Source Notes: Anthropic Provider and Supply Chain Risk

*Reviewed April 28, 2026*

These notes capture external context relevant to model-provider dependency risk for a government-facing prototype.

Defense News / Associated Press reported on February 27, 2026 that the Trump administration ordered U.S. agencies to stop using Anthropic technology, and that Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth said he was designating Anthropic as a supply chain risk. Source: https://www.defensenews.com/news/pentagon-congress/2026/02/27/trump-orders-federal-agencies-to-stop-using-anthropic-technology-in-dispute-over-ai-safety/

Nextgov/FCW reported on March 2, 2026 that multiple federal agencies had begun phasing out Anthropic tools following the White House directive. Source: https://www.nextgov.com/acquisition/2026/03/agencies-begin-shed-anthropic-contracts-following-trumps-directive/411823/

Anthropic's public Claude status page shows that Claude services can experience degraded performance, elevated errors, and outages across specific products or model families. Source: https://status.claude.com/

This source should be treated as external operational and procurement context, not as a claim about model quality.
